Road of
the Dead

South London, weeks after the outbreak. Alex Morgan's battered sedan is running on fumes. Cross Tower Bridge, run the gauntlet down Tower Bridge Road, refuel under pressure at the Roadstar off the Old Kent Road, break through the overrun army checkpoint and the pile-up beyond, and hold the staging-yard gate long enough to make the last convoy to Dover. Streets, buildings, trees and street furniture follow the real map. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

Out of London

⛽ Fuel The tank is nearly dry. Reach the petrol station off Dunton Road (follow the yellow beacon). Park beside a pump, get out (F) and hold E. Red canisters on the map add +6 L.
🔧 Scrap & repairs Wrecks with a 🔧 marker can be searched: get out and hold E next to them. Scrap repairs the sedan: stand next to it on foot and hold E — 2 scrap restores +18% to the most damaged part (body, engine or wheels).
🔫 Weapons The crowbar is always in hand (Click / J). The pistol fires with Right-click / K — loud, it draws the horde. Find the shotgun in the police car on Tower Bridge Road or the army crate at the Old Kent Road checkpoint (marked 🔫). Q swaps, R reloads.
✚ Health Medkits come from searched wrecks; G heals. Space dodges on foot, Shift sprints.
🚗 Driving You can mount kerbs and plough through signs, lamps, bollards and bus shelters — small props cost little damage, heavy ones (post boxes, phone boxes, shelters) cost a lot. Infected clinging to the car are shaken off by swerving.
☣ Infected Runners sprint, brutes shrug off the crowbar, and screamers call the whole street if you let them finish — kill them first.

Textures, sky HDRI (belfast_sunset_puresky) and 30+ street props: Poly Haven (CC0). Car, characters, animation and sound effects generated for this project with Higgsfield (Meshy, Mirelo).
